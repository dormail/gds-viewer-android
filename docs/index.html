GDS Viewer

An Android app for viewing GDSII chip layouts

GDS Viewer opens .gds and .gds.gz layout files on Android phones and tablets. You can pan, zoom, rotate, show or hide layers, choose a cell and measure distances with a ruler. Layouts of a few hundred MB open in seconds, and everything happens on the phone.
